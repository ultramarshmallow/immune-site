Immune

Your lock screen isn’t the problem. The junk that gets there is.

Immune is an iPhone app that stops junk in Gmail — and texts from numbers you don’t know — from becoming a ping. It does not sit in front of your lock screen.

How it works

  1. Connect one Gmail account.
  2. Immune watches what you archive or ignore (it does not invent junk history from a silent whole-mailbox scan).
  3. When the same pattern shows up enough times, it asks: Block this in the future?
  4. If you say yes, future matching mail skips the inbox, matching mail already in the inbox gets cleaned, and Immune unsubscribes when a List-Unsubscribe header exists. You can undo any rule.

Platforms

iPhone · iOS 17+ · Gmail + unknown-sender SMS
