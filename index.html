Immune

Your lock screen isn’t the problem. The junk that gets there is.

Immune does not sit in front of your lock screen. It stops junk in Gmail — and texts from numbers you don’t know — from becoming a ping. When you say yes to a rule, it also cleans matching mail already in your inbox.

How it works

  1. Connect Gmail — Immune watches what you archive or ignore. It does not invent a junk history from a silent scan of your whole mailbox.
  2. It asks before it blocks — After the same pattern shows up a few times, you get one question: Block this in the future? Yes / No / Always allow.
  3. Yes cleans forward and back — Future matching mail skips the inbox. Matching mail already sitting there gets cleaned. If the sender supports List-Unsubscribe, Immune unsubscribes. You can undo any rule.
What we are not
  • Not a lock-screen filter for every app
  • Not Slack, Instagram, or iMessage-from-contacts
  • Not an LLM reading your mail to rewrite it
  • Not another dashboard you have to live in

Safety

OTP codes, banks, travel, and first-time important senders stay off auto-suggest. When in doubt, Immune does not suggest. Prefer missing a filter over blocking something real.

SMS

Unknown-sender texts only, on your phone, via Apple’s Message Filter. Setup: Messages → Unknown & Spam → Immune. Contacts’ iMessages are out of scope.

Privacy

Gmail filters live in your Gmail account. SMS rules stay on your device. No vendor LLM. No third-party analytics in the product promise. Undo anytime.
