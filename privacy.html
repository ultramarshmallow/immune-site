Privacy Policy — Immune

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Product: Immune (iOS)

Contact: yz611999@gmail.com

What Immune is

Immune helps you reduce junk notifications from Gmail and SMS from unknown senders. It does not intercept other apps’ lock-screen notifications.

Data we access

  • Google Sign-In / Gmail: with your permission, Immune uses Google OAuth scopes to read limited mailbox metadata (such as headers and snippets), observe inbox actions needed to learn junk patterns, create and manage Gmail filters/labels you approve, and apply those actions to matching mail already in your inbox when you accept a rule. When a message includes a standard List-Unsubscribe header, Immune may send an unsubscribe request you triggered by accepting a rule.
  • SMS (unknown senders only): filtering runs on your iPhone via Apple’s Message Filter extension. Message content for filtering stays on device per Apple’s model; Immune does not upload your SMS to a vendor LLM.
  • No third-party analytics SDKs in the MVP. No selling of personal data.

What we do not do

  • We do not read or mute other apps’ notifications.
  • We do not filter iMessage from your contacts, Slack, Outlook, or Instagram.
  • We do not use your mail to train a cloud AI model.
  • We do not auto-block safety classes such as OTPs, banks, and similar without your control model described in the app (fail safe: miss rather than over-block).

Storage

  • Google tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain on your device.
  • Learning state and rules live on device (and Gmail filters you accept live in your Gmail account).
  • Disconnecting Immune removes local access tokens and local history as described in the app; Gmail filters already created remain until you delete them in Immune Rules or Gmail settings.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with:

  • Google (Gmail API / Sign-In) as needed to provide the service you requested
  • Apple (App Store / push / OS filtering) as required to run on iOS

Your choices

  • Accept, reject, or always-allow suggestions
  • Undo or delete rules
  • Disconnect Google and delete local Immune data
  • Manage or delete Gmail filters in Gmail settings

Children

Immune is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. We will revise the date above when we do.
